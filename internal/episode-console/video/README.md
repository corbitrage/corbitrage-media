# Episode 1 video source (draft)

- `scene.html`: animated SVG scenes (Sippu = chai glass, Chinnu = pink saree), time-driven by `render(t)`.
- `render.js`: Playwright frame renderer (540x960 at 2x = 1080x1920, 24 fps, 36.1 s).
- Fonts: `npm i @fontsource/noto-sans-telugu` next to scene.html (referenced by relative path).
- Voices: Google TTS via Speko, Telugu. Sippu = Charon, Chinnu = Sulafat. Lines are drafts: native-speaker review required before release.
- Mux: audio lines placed at 1.0, 8.8, 12.2, 18.0, 29.0 s, loudnorm -16 LUFS, H.264 + AAC.
- The video itself is not committed. Not for publishing until owner approval.

Privacy Policy

Corbitrage Private Limited

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Corbitrage Private Limited ("Corbitrage", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy of visitors and individuals who contact us through our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information submitted through our website and related communication channels.

1. Information we may collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your WhatsApp or mobile number.
  • Your email address, if you choose to give it.
  • Your selected enquiry category or financial-service interest.
  • The contents of your enquiry.
  • Your consent and communication preferences.

We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type, device information, IP address and website usage information, where applicable.

Please do not submit PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or other highly sensitive information through the general enquiry form.

2. Why we use your information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your enquiries.
  • Contact you regarding services you have requested.
  • Coordinate mutual fund distribution-related assistance.
  • Maintain enquiry and communication records.
  • Improve website performance and customer experience.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.
  • Protect our website against spam, fraud and unauthorised activity.

We will not treat submission of a general enquiry as permission to execute an investment transaction or initiate an investment on your behalf.

3. Consent and communication

Where consent is required, we will request it through a clear and affirmative action. Consent to be contacted about your enquiry is separate from optional marketing consent, which you may decline without affecting your enquiry.

You may withdraw consent for optional communications by contacting us at the email address below. Withdrawal will not affect processing already lawfully undertaken before withdrawal, or retention required by applicable law.

4. Sharing of information

We may share relevant information with service providers engaged to support our business operations, such as:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Cloudflare, for website delivery and security.
  • Make.com, if used to process website enquiries.
  • CRM or business communication platforms, if configured.
  • Email and communication service providers.

Such service providers should process information only for authorised business purposes and subject to appropriate contractual and security safeguards.

We may also disclose information where required by law, a competent authority or a valid legal process.

We do not sell personal information as a business activity.

5. Data retention

Our standard retention period for website enquiries is 12 months after the last contact, unless:

  • The enquiry becomes an active customer or business relationship.
  • A longer period is required by applicable law or regulatory obligations.
  • The information is reasonably required for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted or anonymised, subject to applicable retention obligations.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your privacy requests

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to:

  • Request information about personal data associated with your enquiry.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing.
  • Raise a privacy-related grievance.

We will review and respond to such requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

8. Children

Our general enquiry form is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Please contact us if you believe that a child's personal information has been submitted without appropriate authorisation.

9. Third-party websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, mutual fund platforms and service providers. Their privacy policies and practices are governed by their own terms. We are not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

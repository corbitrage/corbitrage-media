Disclosures

Who we are

Corbitrage Private Limited is an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor, ARN-336411. ARN validity: ADD validity date from ARN certificate. We are not a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser and we do not provide investment advisory services.

How we earn (commission transparency)

We earn commission from mutual fund houses (asset management companies) for the schemes we distribute. This is usually a trail commission paid from the scheme's expense ratio, calculated as a percentage of the value of the investments you hold through us. This means our income can rise or fall with the value of your holdings and with how long you stay invested.

Regular plans and direct plans

Regular plans include distributor commission and therefore have a slightly higher expense ratio than direct plans of the same scheme. Direct plans do not include distributor commission, but you then manage selection, paperwork and follow-up yourself. Please compare both and decide what suits you.

Our remuneration and commission disclosure

Corbitrage Private Limited may receive distribution commission from AMCs or other authorised distribution platforms in connection with mutual fund investments made through its distribution services.

The nature and amount of commission may vary depending on the mutual fund scheme, plan, transaction and applicable arrangements.

Investors should understand that regular plans generally include distribution expenses in their expense ratio, whereas direct plans do not include distributor commission.

Our remuneration may create a potential conflict of interest. We aim to disclose relevant conflicts transparently and conduct our distribution activities in accordance with applicable regulations and the AMFI Code of Conduct.

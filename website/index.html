How we verify financial information

We aim to make the source and context of information clear, so you can check what a figure means before relying on it.

Source first

For mutual fund NAV information, we identify AMFI as the data source and link to its official NAV facilities. Scheme documents and AMC disclosures should be checked for scheme-specific details.

Date and context

Check the NAV date and the retrieval date where displayed. NAV is not an intraday market price, and a change in NAV alone does not show an investor's total return.

Assumptions made visible

Calculator outputs depend on the amount, duration and assumed rate entered. These are illustrations only; actual market-linked outcomes can be lower, higher or negative.

How to read index figures

An index level is not the return of any mutual fund. You cannot invest in an index directly, many headline indices exclude dividends, and a fund’s result can differ because of costs, strategy and timing. Index history does not predict future results.

For scheme-level performance, read the fund house’s official factsheet and scheme documents, or use AMFI’s official NAV facility. Links go to third-party websites that Corbitrage does not control.
